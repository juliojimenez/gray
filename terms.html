Terms, in plain words

Effective November 14, 2026

The short version: the Foundations course is free and stays free. The subscription is US$5 a month and unlocks every paid learning path, the ones here today and the ones still to come. You can cancel whenever you like, and payments aren't refunded.

Who's who

Gray is a small independent project that lives at gray.academy. On this page “we” means Gray, and there is a real person behind it who reads julio@julioj.com. “You” means the grown-up who subscribes.

What's free

The Foundations course, all eleven sections of it, is free in the browser and as a download. It needs no account and no payment, and nothing on this page about money applies to it.

What the subscription gets you

While your subscription is running, every paid learning path on gray.academy is unlocked: the paths that exist today, like The Game Maker, and any we add later, at no extra charge. Paid paths run in the browser only.

One subscription covers your household. Use it on as many of your family's computers as you like, but please don't pass the key on to other families or post it online.

Price and billing

The subscription costs US$5 per month. Checkout is handled by Stripe, which shows the exact total before you pay. Depending on where you live, that total can include sales tax or VAT and may be shown in your own currency.

The subscription renews by itself each month, and your card is charged each time, until you cancel.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, and once you have, you won't be charged again. The paid paths stay unlocked until the end of the month you've already paid for.

Your receipt email comes with a way to manage or cancel the subscription yourself. If you can't find it, write to julio@julioj.com from the address you subscribed with and we'll cancel it for you.

Refunds

Payments aren't refunded, and that includes a month you've only partly used. If you cancel, you keep access until that month runs out. If the law where you live gives you a right to a refund, this page doesn't take it away.

Who can subscribe

Subscribing is a grown-up's job. You need to be at least 18, or the age of majority where you live. Kids: if you'd like the paid paths, ask a parent or guardian.

Your subscription key

Right after checkout, Gray shows you a subscription key and saves it in that browser, so the paid paths simply open there. Keep a copy of the key somewhere safe, because it's what unlocks the paths on another computer or after the browser's data has been cleared.

If you lose it, write to julio@julioj.com from the address you subscribed with and we'll send it again.

If something breaks

Gray is made with care, but it comes as it is, and we can't promise it will always be online or free of bugs. If a paid path isn't working, tell us at julio@julioj.com and we'll fix it as quickly as we can.

Questions or changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions are welcome at julio@julioj.com. How Gray treats your data is on the privacy page.
